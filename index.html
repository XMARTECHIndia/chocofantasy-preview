OUR STORY

Story ofChocofantasy

ChocoFantasy began with a simple belief: chocolate tastes better when someone has taken the time to make it. Every piece is shaped, filled and finished by hand, in small batches, in Kolkata — grown from gifts for family into a name trusted for festive hampers, team gifting and client relationships across India.
