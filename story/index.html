Who we are

Chocolate making is patient, careful work. Choco Fantasy was built around that care, so every piece that leaves our kitchen is one we would be proud to give ourselves.

Choco Fantasy began in January 2017 with Miss Tahira Sarkar and a simple promise: the best handmade chocolate for Kolkata first, and then for the whole country. What started as her start-up is still her passion, and today it is shared by everyone who works at the bench beside her.

01

Our mission

To deliver the best quality handmade chocolate treats to our city, and gradually to the entire country.

02

Our vision

To become a leading national chocolatier known for excellence, innovation and the most loving chocolates.

How it happened

From one kitchen to every occasion

01 / 04The spark

Spark

01The spark

It began with one stubborn idea

Chocolate should taste like someone truly cared about it. When Tahira could not find the chocolate she imagined, she decided to make it herself, slowly and properly, in a small kitchen in Kolkata.

That kitchen became Choco Fantasy.

Batch

02The first batch

Made slowly, and made by hand

The first chocolates were tempered, filled and finished by hand and shared with the people closest to us. They were gone in a day.

We still work the same way: the finest ingredients we can find, careful methods at every step, and a taste of every recipe before it leaves the kitchen.

Gifts

03Word spreads

One box turned into every celebration

Friends asked for boxes for birthdays and festivals. Then their friends did. Soon we were packing chocolate for weddings, anniversaries, Rakhi, Valentine’s Day and every sweet occasion in between.

Many of our flavours began as a customer’s craving or a late-night experiment.

Today

04Today

Trusted by families and teams across India

Today Choco Fantasy makes dark, milk and white chocolate, truffles, fruit and nut bars, premium gift boxes and hampers, and takes bulk orders for corporate gifting and promotional events.

The scale has grown. The care has not changed.
